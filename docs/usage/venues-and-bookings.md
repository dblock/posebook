# Venues, Sessions, and Bookings

[Back to Posebook](../../README.md)

Anyone with a venue link can view its address, photos, Instagram, and recurring sessions. A session link opens that program's regular schedule and public calendar. Venue and session lists and booking actions require sign-in and the appropriate access. Public calendars show only active models' public names and opted-in Instagram handles; private profiles appear as **To be announced**.

As a model, choose an available date at a session to book a drawing. You can have one upcoming booking per recurring session, including multiple sessions at the same venue. Invitations are session-specific, with no additional cross-session date/time conflict restriction. Use **Change Date** or **Cancel Booking** on your booking to update plans, and provide a short explanation when prompted. Dates and times follow the session's time zone.

Location pages use `/venues/:slug`; calendars and bookings use `/sessions/:slug`. Previous slugs remain valid within those paths. Old root shortcuts and venue-nested calendar/booking links are not redirected.

Starting seven days before your drawing, confirm that you will attend. Until five days before the drawing, you can report that you can no longer make it and follow the rescheduling steps. After that cutoff, contact an organizer to reschedule; you can still confirm attendance or cancel.
