# Venues and Bookings

[Back to Posebook](../../README.md)

Anyone with a venue link can view its address, regular schedule, and public calendar. Venue lists and booking actions require sign-in and the appropriate venue access. Public calendars show only active models' public names and opted-in Instagram handles; private profiles appear as **To be announced**.

As a model, choose an available date at a venue to book a drawing. You can have one upcoming booking per venue. Use **Change Date** or **Cancel Booking** on your booking to update plans, and provide a short explanation when prompted. Dates and times follow the venue's time zone.

Starting seven days before your drawing, confirm that you will attend. Until five days before the drawing, you can report that you can no longer make it and follow the rescheduling steps. After that cutoff, contact an organizer to reschedule; you can still confirm attendance or cancel.
