# Accounts and Profiles

[Back to Posebook](../../README.md)

Sign in with your email and password, or use the password-reset link if you cannot sign in. Registration is invitation-based after the first administrator account has been created.

Venue/session coordinator invitations are restricted to the invited email address. Register with that address or sign in to the matching existing account, then accept the invitation. If signed in to a different account, use **Sign Out and Use the Invited Account** to switch without losing the invitation. Participant invitations retain their existing personal-link behavior and are not restricted to a specified email.

Use **Profile** to edit your name, display name, phone, Instagram handle, and privacy preferences. Your display name defaults to your account name. Instagram appears on public calendars only when tagging is enabled; **Keep all my information private** hides your name and Instagram from public pages while retaining your preferences.

Use **Change Password** or **Deactivate Account** from your profile to manage account access. Changing your password signs out other sessions. Deactivation signs you out and retains your booking history.
