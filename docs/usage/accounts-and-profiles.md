# Accounts and Profiles

[Back to Posebook](../../README.md)

Sign in with your email and password, or use the password-reset link if you cannot sign in. After the first administrator account, registration requires an invitation or email verification for a program accepting participation applications.

On an open program, choose **Apply to Participate**. Sign in with a verified account, or request a 30-minute verification link and create/verify your account. Prepare your account details and photos before explicitly submitting the application and profile-sharing consent. Applying does not enroll you or permit booking. The program's reviewing coordinators can see your private contact details, gender, profile, and photos while the application is Pending or Not Now.

Use **Profile > Applications** to see your own status and history. Coordinators receive a submission email, and you receive an acceptance or courteous **Not Now** email. **Not Now** means the program is not accepting you right now and can reconsider later without a new submission. You can **Withdraw Application** before acceptance to prevent future acceptance and end application-only review and future updates; retained history remains, and existing participant access is unchanged. A withdrawn application cannot be reopened; ask an organizer for an invitation if you later wish to participate. Reviewer notes are never shown to applicants or included in their email. Email may be delayed; the application page always shows the current status.

Password-reset and email-change instructions are queued for email delivery. Their links expire 30 minutes after the request, not after delivery; requesting new instructions invalidates the previous link. Profile email changes keep your current login address until you follow the verification link. If email is delayed or expired, request new instructions or contact an organizer.

Participant and venue/program coordinator invitations follow the same email rules. If the sender specified an email, register with that address or sign in to the matching existing account, then accept the invitation. If signed in to a different account, use **Sign Out and Use the Invited Account** to switch without losing the invitation. When no email was specified, one recipient can accept the private link with any account.

Open **Profile** and choose the **Edit** tab to change your name, display name, phone, Instagram handle, and privacy preferences. Your display name defaults to your account name. Registration forms select Instagram tagging by default; enter a handle or clear the tagging checkbox. Instagram appears on public calendars only when tagging is enabled; **Keep all my information private** hides your name and Instagram from public pages while retaining saved preferences.

Phone and gender are required for all accounts. Phone numbers may include spaces, parentheses, periods, or dashes; ten-digit numbers are treated as US/Canada, and other numbers need a country code. Omit an optional trunk prefix such as `(0)`, or enter a significant zero without parentheses. Phone numbers are unverified and cannot be used for recovery or messaging. Gender is private. Existing accounts may have a migration placeholder for either field; check and update your profile.

Open the profile's **Security** tab for the **Change Password** and **Deactivate Account** forms. Each form is submitted separately. Changing your password signs out other sessions. Deactivation signs you out and retains your booking history. System administrator accounts cannot deactivate themselves.

Open your profile through your account name in the header. Its default **View** lists your current bookings and programs you can book with an active invitation. **History** contains your retained bookings across all programs, including cancellations and completed sessions; use **Previous** and **Next** to browse its 50-booking pages. The **Account Details** tab contains contact, sharing, and pending email-verification information without a photo gallery; saving or canceling profile edits returns there. Use **Photos** to upload, delete, or change sharing preferences, with no photo-count cap. A vertical separator groups **Edit** and **Security** apart from information tabs. Administrators have a top-level **Users** link, not a profile tab. Click a photo in any profile, venue, or program gallery to preview the larger image without leaving the page. Close the preview with **Close**, Escape, or a click outside it.

On **Edit Profile**, use **Set Primary Photo** to choose the thumbnail shown beside your name in organizers' model tables. Administrators can do the same on **Edit User**. The first uploaded photo is used by default, and deleting a primary photo falls back to the first remaining photo. Choosing a primary photo never makes it public. Model names and emails in private tables link to their profile and photos; Instagram accounts also appear in model selectors and private table rows.

Use **Copy** beside contact details, Instagram handles, venue addresses, and venue/program links to copy the displayed value or share the page. Private invitation links have a **Copy** button inside the link box while first shown; keep those links private. The button changes to **Copied** for three seconds after copying, then returns to **Copy**. If your browser denies clipboard access, a message asks you to select and copy manually.
