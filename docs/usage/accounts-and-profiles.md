# Accounts and Profiles

[Back to Posebook](../../README.md)

Sign in with your email and password, or use the password-reset link if you cannot sign in. After the first administrator account, registration requires an invitation or email verification for a program accepting participation applications.

On an open program, choose **Apply to Participate**. Sign in with a verified account, or request a 30-minute verification link and create/verify your account. The application editor brings account details, photos, and your message together on one page. **Save Draft** creates a private application on the first save, even with incomplete details, so you can return through **Profile > Applications**. Account edits are staged in the draft and applied only when you submit; photo uploads are saved to your account immediately and are public by default. Selecting photos uploads them and saves a private draft without submitting the application. Uploads and draft saves update in place; without JavaScript, choose **Upload Photos** and use ordinary page submissions.

Your signed-in homepage and **Profile > Applications** show your applications in the same table with their statuses. **View** shows the program, read-only account details, photos, message, and history, including for drafts. Use **Continue Application** to edit a draft or **Cancel Application** to discard it. **Withdraw** is available for pending and rejected applications. You retain access to your own withdrawn application and private photos. Submitting or canceling an application returns you to **Profile > Applications**.

Management homepages also show a paginated **Submitted Applications** table with the program, applicant, status, and a **View** link to review. The **Status** filter defaults to **Pending**; choose **Reject**, **Accepted**, **Withdrawn**, or **All** to see other submissions. On the homepage and program applications page, filtering and pagination update the application list in place without reloading the document; the URL and browser Back/Forward follow the selected view. Without JavaScript, choose a status and press **Filter**. System administrators see submissions across all programs; coordinators see only directly assigned or inherited programs. Drafts are excluded from every filter, including applications reopened for editing.

**Resubmit**, available in the table and on a withdrawn application's detail page, reopens only a Withdrawn application as a private draft when the program is active and accepting applications. It preserves your previous message and history, sends no coordinator notification, and requires fresh sharing consent before submission. The reopened draft has a new 30-day editing period. Canceling it or letting it expire restores the previous withdrawn submission rather than deleting its history. Drafts remain listed and can be canceled when intake is closed.

History records draft creation, submission, review decisions, withdrawal, reopening, cancellation, and expiry, displayed newest first. Repeated cycles add separate entries; ordinary saves do not change state. Draft-only transitions remain private from coordinators, including after submission. Canceling or expiring an initial draft deletes its contents but retains a minimal state-change record.

Drafts grant no membership, booking access, coordinator review, or coordinator notifications. **Cancel Application** removes only the unfinished application, keeping your account and uploaded photos. **Submit Application** requires valid account details and explicit profile-sharing consent; with JavaScript, the button stays disabled until consent is checked. Submitted applications retain their history; the program's reviewing coordinators can see your private contact details, gender, profile, and photos while the application is pending or rejected.

We send one completion reminder after seven days while your account is active and verified and the program still accepts applications. Sign in through the reminder to complete or abandon the draft; opening the link does neither automatically. Unfinished drafts are removed after 30 days from the first save, even if you edit them later. Cleanup removes only the draft and staged account edits, not your account, photos, or submitted history. Reminder delivery and scheduled cleanup depend on background-worker availability.

Organizers can also share a direct `/programs/<program-slug>/apply` link to the same workflow. Applications must be enabled for that specific program, and both the program and venue must be active. Opening the link does not submit an application or grant access.

Accepting a participant invitation or having your application accepted notifies the program's assigned coordinators when email is configured. These alerts include your name and acceptance source, not your contact details or private reviewer notes. If notifications cannot be queued, acceptance is not saved; retry later. Your existing booking rules and any pause remain unchanged.

Use **Profile > Applications** to see your own status and history. Coordinators receive a submission email, and you receive an acceptance or courteous rejection email. **Reject** means the program is not accepting you right now and can reconsider later without a new submission. You can **Withdraw Application** before acceptance to prevent future acceptance and end application-only review and future updates; retained history remains, and existing participant access is unchanged. Reviewer notes are never shown to applicants or included in their email. Email may be delayed; the application page always shows the current status.

Password-reset and email-change instructions are queued for email delivery. Their links expire 30 minutes after the request, not after delivery; requesting new instructions invalidates the previous link. Profile email changes keep your current login address until you follow the verification link. If email is delayed or expired, request new instructions or contact an organizer.

Participant and venue/program coordinator invitations follow the same email rules. If the sender specified an email, register with that address or sign in to the matching existing account, then accept the invitation. If signed in to a different account, use **Sign Out and Use the Invited Account** to switch without losing the invitation. When no email was specified, one recipient can accept the private link with any account.

Open **Profile** and choose the **Edit** tab to change your name, display name, phone, Instagram handle, and profile preferences. Public pages show only your display name, never your account name; without one they show "To be announced". Registration forms select Instagram tagging by default; enter a handle or clear the tagging checkbox. Instagram appears publicly only when tagging is enabled and your account is active. Only active accounts show photos marked Public.

Phone and gender are required for all accounts. Phone numbers may include spaces, parentheses, periods, or dashes; ten-digit numbers are treated as US/Canada, and other numbers need a country code. Omit an optional trunk prefix such as `(0)`, or enter a significant zero without parentheses. Phone numbers are unverified and cannot be used for recovery or messaging. Gender is private. Existing accounts may have a migration placeholder for either field; check and update your profile.

Open the profile's **Security** tab for the **Change Password** and **Deactivate Account** forms. Each form is submitted separately. Changing your password signs out other sessions.

To leave Posebook, choose **Deactivate My Account** and confirm your current password. This signs you out on all devices and prevents sign-in, password recovery, and new bookings. Your name, Instagram, and photos are no longer shared publicly while inactive. Your account, photos, memberships, and booking history are retained privately for authorized administrators and organizers, not deleted. There is no self-service account deletion, and personal information is not sold.

Deactivation does not cancel bookings. Contact your organizer about upcoming bookings before deactivating. Existing private calendar invitations may continue to be updated, and information already sent by email or to Google Calendar cannot be recalled here. A system administrator can reactivate your account, restoring saved sharing preferences. System administrator accounts cannot deactivate themselves. See the hosted service's **Privacy Policy** for retention details and privacy requests.

Open your profile through your account name in the header. Its default **View** lists your current bookings and programs you can book with an active invitation. **History** contains your retained bookings across all programs, including cancellations and completed sessions; use **Previous** and **Next** to browse its 50-booking pages. The **Account Details** tab contains contact, sharing, and pending email-verification information without a photo gallery; saving or canceling profile edits returns there. Use **Photos** to upload, delete, or change sharing preferences, with no photo-count cap. A vertical separator groups **Edit** and **Security** apart from information tabs. Administrators have a top-level **Users** link, not a profile tab. Click a photo in any profile, venue, or program gallery to preview the larger image without leaving the page. Close the preview with **Close**, Escape, or a click outside it.

In **Photos**, choosing a file or dropping photos onto the upload form uploads them automatically in account, venue, program, and administrator galleries. Without JavaScript, choose **Upload Photo** after selecting a file. Use **Make Primary** to choose the thumbnail shown beside your name in organizers' model tables. Administrators can do the same from a user's **Profile**. Choosing a primary photo also makes it public. Without a choice, or after deleting the primary photo or making it private, the first public photo is used; if no photos are public, none is primary. Model names in calendars and private tables link to their public profile, which shows everyone, including organizers, only the public name, permitted Instagram link, next visible booking, and public photos; inactive accounts have no public profile. Coordinators also see **Edit Model** under calendar bookings; Instagram accounts also appear in model selectors and private table rows.

Use **Copy** beside contact details, Instagram handles, venue addresses, and venue/program links to copy the displayed value or share the page. Private invitation links have a **Copy** button inside the link box while first shown; keep those links private. The button changes to **Copied** for three seconds after copying, then returns to **Copy**. If your browser denies clipboard access, a message asks you to select and copy manually.
