# Organizing Venues and Programs

[Back to Posebook](../../README.md)

System administrators create physical venues, invite or promote **Venue Coordinators**, and manage accounts. Venue coordinators edit their location, photos, and Instagram, create recurring programs, manage every program there, and invite or promote **Program Coordinators**. Coordinator assignment does not expose a global user directory.

Your homepage lists **Programs You Manage**, with **Manage Calendar** opening each program's organizer calendar. Venue coordinators see every program at their venues; program coordinators see only their assigned programs; system administrators see all programs. Inactive programs and programs at inactive venues remain listed for management. Accounts that are also participants see their eligible programs separately under **Programs You Can Book**.

In program **Edit**, enable **Accept Participation Applications** to let people apply; it is off by default. The **Applications** tab lists this program's applications, including a **Not Now** filter for future reconsideration. Review the submitted message and consented current profile/photos, then choose **Accept** or **Not Now**, optionally recording private reviewer notes. Accept enrolls the applicant through the same initial booking-grant path as an accepted invitation without renewing an existing cycle or clearing a pause. Closing intake retains applications and permits later acceptance while the program/account remain active. Withdrawn applications cannot be accepted.

**Participation History** distinguishes application submissions/decisions from accepted invitations and retains each decision, actor, time, and manager-only notes. Applicant identities are retained from submission, not refreshed after withdrawal. Application-only private-photo access ends on withdrawal; reviewing coordinators cannot edit photos.

In the participant roster, choose **Edit Participant** (or the configured participant label) to update **Account Name**, **Public Display Name**, and **Coordinator Notes**. Names change across all programs; the account name is the public fallback when no display name is set, and Private Identity still hides public names. Notes are private to this program's coordinators and administrators, including inherited venue coordinators; the participant and other programs cannot see them. Notes support multiple lines, are displayed as plain text, and can be cleared by leaving the field blank.

Use the venue's **Edit**, **Photos**, **Create Program**, and **Coordinators** tabs for permitted management actions. Programs provide **Edit** and **Google Calendar** tabs alongside Calendar, History, and participant/coordinator management. Breadcrumbs show the current venue, program, and section.

System administrators can **Deactivate Venue** or **Activate Venue** in venue **Edit**. Venue coordinators and system administrators can deactivate or activate individual programs in program **Edit**; program coordinators cannot. Deactivation hides the location from public pages and galleries and prevents new bookings and date changes, including organizer scheduling. Managers and participants with upcoming bookings retain access. Existing bookings, attendance confirmations, cancellations, history, and calendar invitations remain intact. Reactivating a venue restores only programs that have not been independently deactivated.

In **Photos**, choose **Set Primary Photo** to select the venue image shown inside participants' upcoming booking cards on the homepage. The first uploaded photo is used by default and when the selected photo is deleted. Selection is available only to system administrators and the venue's coordinators; photos are public while the venue is active.

Venue **Programs** lists only programs at that venue. Each program has its own public **Photos** tab, where its authorized program/venue coordinators and admins can upload, delete, and choose **Set Primary Photo** independently of venue photos. Program cards use the selected program photo, or the venue photo when no program photo exists. All account, venue, and program galleries have no photo-count cap; file-size, pixel-dimension, and content checks still protect uploads. Admin account management is available through the profile's **Users** tab.

In venue **Edit**, optionally paste a **Google Maps Link** for the exact place or map location. Standard `google.com/maps`, `maps.google.com`, `maps.app.goo.gl`, and `goo.gl/maps` HTTPS links are supported. Without a custom link, **View on Google Maps** searches for the venue's address. The link is public on venue/program pages and included with the venue name and address in Google Calendar invitations. Existing upcoming invitations pick up address/link changes through the worker's five-minute reconciliation.

On **Venue Coordinators** or **Program Coordinators**, leave email blank and choose **Continue** to generate a private link for one person, or enter an email to restrict acceptance to that address. If that email already has an active account, Posebook offers **Grant Coordinator Access** for confirmation instead of creating another account. Their other memberships and booking permissions are retained. For a new account, create a private registration link and optionally queue it for email delivery. No email is sent when the address is blank. The link expires in 30 days and can be revoked from the coordinator list. Copy it when created; it is not displayed again.

Venue coordinator access includes all programs at that venue; program coordinator access includes only that program. Neither promotion grants system admin access. An outstanding invitation stops working if its sender becomes inactive or loses the authority to delegate that venue/program.

Use **Create Program** on a venue for a recurring program. New programs default to Mondays at 9 AM UTC, no end time, and zero pay; choose the actual weekdays, times, time zone, pay, and instructions in settings. An optional end time earlier than the start means the next day. Use **Manage Schedule** to set or clear times for a single date. End times determine booking completion; without one, bookings complete at the end of the local date. Each program has separate rules, invitations, roster, and calendar. Program coordinators edit only their assigned programs and cannot edit the venue, manage siblings, or assign coordinator roles. Venue coordinators inherit those capabilities across all programs at their venue.

Program settings include **Program Label** and **Participant Label**, defaulting to **Program** and **Participant**. Use singular labels such as **Workshop** and **Instructor** to specialize the interface. Calendars, rosters, invitations, and booking controls use those labels; **Pay** stays unchanged. Labels do not change booking rules: one person per date, one upcoming booking per person per program, and organizer permission for repeat bookings. Gender rules are optional and disabled by default; all accounts require a gender selection. Attendee registration is not implemented.

Use **Calendar invitation lead time (minutes)** in program settings to make Google Calendar invitations begin before the actual session. The default is 0; 15 makes a 10:00 session's invitation start at 9:45. This changes only Google event start times, not session times, booking history, attendance deadlines, or registration invitation emails. Existing upcoming calendar invitations are updated when this setting changes. See [Google Calendar Setup](../google-calendar.md).

Set **First bookable date (optional)** under program **Edit** to open online booking only for sessions on or after that date. For example, enter January 1, 2027 while continuing to manage earlier dates manually: invited participants can accept invitations and book January onward immediately. The calendar initially opens that month, but earlier upcoming sessions remain visible through month navigation. The booking horizon counts calendar months from the later of today or the first bookable date, using the program's time zone; a six-month horizon starting January 1 ends July 1, inclusive. Once that date passes, the horizon rolls forward from today as usual. Leave the date blank to retain today's rolling window. New bookings and date changes, including organizer scheduling, respect the cutoff; existing bookings, attendance confirmations, cancellations, history, and actual session schedules are not rewritten.

Program coordinators use the program navigation to open the calendar, booking history, and participant roster and invitations. From the calendar they can schedule or update bookings, confirm attendance, and manage dated schedule exceptions. Venue coordinators and system admins can also open the program's coordinator page. Pending participant invitations can be revoked beside their status on the roster page.

Invitations provide private, single-use registration links with the same optional-email policy for participants and coordinators. A specified email restricts acceptance to that account; leave it blank for a link one recipient can accept with any account. A participant must accept an invitation to join a program. Joining grants an initial booking; coordinators can invite participants to book again after they complete a booking. A paused participant can keep an existing booking and cancel it, but cannot book or reschedule until resumed. Memberships and invitations are independent across programs.

Email is used for account recovery and optional invitation delivery. Posebook does not yet send automated booking reminders or booking notifications.
