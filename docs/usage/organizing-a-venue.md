# Organizing a Venue

[Back to Posebook](../../README.md)

Venue organizers can invite models, review their venue's roster and booking history, schedule or update bookings, confirm attendance, and manage dated schedule exceptions when a drawing is added, moved, or closed. System administrators configure venues, assign organizers, and manage accounts.

Invitations provide private, single-use registration links. A model must accept an invitation to join a venue. Joining grants an initial booking; organizers can invite models to book again after they complete a drawing. A paused model can keep an existing booking and cancel it, but cannot book or reschedule until resumed.

Email is used for account recovery and optional invitation delivery. Posebook does not yet send automated drawing reminders or booking notifications.
