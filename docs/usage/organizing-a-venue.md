# Organizing Venues and Programs

[Back to Posebook](../../README.md)

System administrators create physical venues, invite or promote **Venue Coordinators**, and manage accounts. Venue coordinators edit their location, photos, and Instagram, create recurring programs, manage every program there, and invite or promote **Program Coordinators**. Coordinator assignment does not expose a global user directory.

System administrators open any account from **Users**. Its pages have the same tabs as your own profile (**Profile**, **Bookings**, **Programs**, **Applications**, **History**, **Edit**, **Photos**, and **Security**). Bookings are view-only, and **Security** offers **Send Password Reset Email** instead of changing the password directly, plus **Deactivate** or **Reactivate** (administrator accounts cannot be deactivated). Opening your own account redirects to your profile.

System administrators can also open **History** in the header or beside **Users** to view recorded account, venue, program, participant-setting, and schedule changes. This global audit history is distinct from an account's booking History. Expand a row's field names to compare **Before** and **After**, follow the actor's account or changed record, and use **Previous**, **Next**, or **First** to navigate. Times are UTC. Deleted records remain readable without a link; **System / Anonymous** means no actor was recorded. History covers only audited writes, not every database operation, and does not provide undo.

Your homepage and profile's **Programs** tab list **Programs You Manage**, with each program name opening its overview and **Manage Calendar** opening its organizer calendar. Venue coordinators see every program at their venues; program coordinators see only their assigned programs; system administrators see all programs. Inactive programs and programs at inactive venues remain listed for management. Accounts that are also participants see their eligible programs under **Programs You Can Book** in the **Bookings** tab.

After signing in, coordinators open their first managed program alphabetically, or their profile's **Programs** tab when they coordinate a venue with no programs. Pending invitation, application, and booking destinations take priority. System administrators retain the public homepage as their default.

In program **Settings > Details**, enable **Accept Participation Applications** to let people apply; it is off by default. First configure email delivery and assign at least one active program or venue coordinator. Each assigned coordinator receives a separate submission email; duplicate assignments receive one message. Global admins are not automatically subscribed, so assign a coordinator role if they should receive notifications. The **Applications** section in the participant group lists this program's applications, including a **Reject** filter for future reconsideration. Review the submitted message and consented current profile/photos, then choose **Accept** or **Reject**, optionally recording private reviewer notes. Applicants receive a decision email without private notes. Accept enrolls the applicant through the same initial booking-grant path as an accepted invitation without renewing an existing cycle or clearing a pause. Closing intake retains applications and permits later acceptance while the program/account remain active. Withdrawn applications cannot be accepted.

**Applications History** distinguishes application submissions/decisions from accepted invitations and retains each decision, actor, time, and manager-only notes. Applicant identities are retained from submission, not refreshed after withdrawal. Application-only private-photo access ends on withdrawal; reviewing coordinators cannot edit photos.

Set **Application Message Placeholder** in program **Settings > Details** to suggest questions such as "Do you have any experience?" inside the empty application message box. It is optional plain text, limited to 5,000 characters, and never becomes the applicant's answer. Leave it blank for no placeholder. Changes are audited and apply to new and saved draft editors without changing existing messages or other programs.

Active assigned program and venue coordinators also receive a separate email when someone accepts a personal participant invitation or a coordinator accepts an application. Dual assignments receive one alert; global administrators must be assigned a coordinator role to receive it. The alert names the participant and acceptance source and links to private **Participation History**, never including contact details or reviewer notes. The applicant's own decision email is unchanged. Delivery rechecks current assignments, participant access, and active accounts/program/venue; revoked or inactive access suppresses pending alerts. Private invitations still work without configured email or an assigned coordinator, but no acceptance alert is queued in those cases. If configured notifications fail to queue, acceptance is not saved and can be retried; SMTP delivery can be delayed or duplicated after acknowledgment failures.

Each recurring program has its own **Accept Participation Applications** toggle; changing one does not change others. Coordinators can find the visible **Application Link** and adjacent **Copy Link** button on the **Program** tab, with an Open or Closed intake label. The link also remains in **Settings > Details**. A shareable URL such as `https://posebook.dblock.org/programs/figure-drawing-mondays-at-enochs/apply` redirects visitors into account preparation or their application form/status without submitting anything. The public **Apply to Participate** button uses the same link. Application links remain clickable when intake is disabled or the program/venue is inactive, showing a generic **Applications Closed** page with a link back to the program. New application forms and submission remain blocked; existing applications remain in **Profile > Applications**. Previous application links continue working after a program slug changes, subject to the same intake setting.

In the participant roster, choose **Edit** to set a **Display Name** for this program, **Hide Instagram Handle**, **Hide Photos** (each disabled when the participant shares nothing to hide), and **Coordinator Notes**. These apply only to this program and never change the participant's own profile; a blank display name falls back to the participant's own display name, or "To be announced". Notes are private to this program's coordinators and administrators, including inherited venue coordinators; the participant and other programs cannot see them. Notes support multiple lines, are displayed as plain text, and can be cleared by leaving the field blank.

The editor shows **User Profile** with what the participant entered and a **Public Card Preview** of what everyone sees on this program: your overrides applied over the profile's public name, permitted Instagram link, and public photos. The preview updates as you type without saving. Inactive accounts stay anonymous, and private photos, contacts, and notes never appear in the public preview. Choose **Save Participant** (or the configured label) to apply changes. Without JavaScript, the preview shows the saved values rendered by the server.

Venue navigation has **Venue**, **Programs**, **Photos**, and authorized **Settings** tabs. In **Settings**, choose **Details**, or the administrator-only **Coordinators** and **Security** sections. **New Program** is at the bottom of the Programs page. **Invite a Coordinator** is at the bottom of program coordinator lists and beside the heading on venue coordinator lists, including when the list is empty.

Program navigation has **Program**, **Calendar**, **Photos**, the configured plural participant label (such as **Models**), and **Settings**, with management groups limited to organizers. The participant group contains **Roster**, **Invitations**, and **Applications**. Program **Settings** contains **Details**, authorized **Coordinators**, **Google Calendar**, and authorized **Security**, in that order. Both the primary group and current section stay highlighted; breadcrumbs show the current venue, program, group, and section.

System administrators can **Deactivate Venue** or **Activate Venue** in venue **Security**. Venue coordinators and system administrators can deactivate or activate individual programs in program **Security**; program coordinators cannot see that tab. Deactivation hides the location from public pages and galleries and prevents new bookings and date changes, including organizer scheduling. Managers and participants with upcoming bookings retain access. Existing bookings, attendance confirmations, cancellations, history, and calendar invitations remain intact. Reactivating a venue restores only programs that have not been independently deactivated.

In **Photos**, choose **Make Primary** to select the venue image shown inside participants' upcoming booking cards in their profile's **Bookings** tab. The first uploaded photo is used by default and when the selected photo is deleted. Selection is available only to system administrators and the venue's coordinators. New photos are public while the venue is active; choose **Make Private** to keep a photo visible only to venue managers. Private photos are never used as the primary photo.

Venue **Programs** lists only programs at that venue. Each program has its own public **Photos** tab, where its authorized program/venue coordinators and admins can upload, delete, **Make Private**/**Make Public**, and choose **Make Primary** independently of venue photos. Private program photos are hidden from guests and never used as the primary photo. Program cards use the selected program photo, or the venue photo when no program photo exists. All account, venue, and program galleries have no photo-count cap; file-size, pixel-dimension, and content checks still protect uploads. Admin account management is available through the profile's **Users** tab.

In venue **Settings > Details**, optionally paste a **Google Maps Link** for the exact place or map location. Standard `google.com/maps`, `maps.google.com`, `maps.app.goo.gl`, and `goo.gl/maps` HTTPS links are supported. Without a custom link, the **Google Maps** link beside the address searches for the venue's address. The link is public on venue/program pages and included with the venue name and address in Google Calendar invitations. Existing upcoming invitations pick up address/link changes through the worker's five-minute reconciliation.

On **Venue Coordinators** or **Program Coordinators**, leave email blank and choose **Continue** to generate a private link for one person, or enter an email to restrict acceptance to that address. If that email already has an active account, Posebook offers **Grant Coordinator Access** for confirmation instead of creating another account. Their other memberships and booking permissions are retained. For a new account, create a private registration link and optionally queue it for email delivery. No email is sent when the address is blank. The link expires in 30 days and can be revoked from the coordinator list. Copy it when created; it is not displayed again.

Venue coordinator access includes all programs at that venue; program coordinator access includes only that program. Neither promotion grants system admin access. An outstanding invitation stops working if its sender becomes inactive or loses the authority to delegate that venue/program.

Use **New Program** on a venue for a recurring program. New programs default to Mondays at 9 AM UTC, no end time, and zero pay; choose the actual weekdays, times, time zone, pay, and instructions in settings. Venue and program descriptions are optional public Markdown, limited to 5,000 characters; program descriptions also appear on application forms. Use private program instructions for member-only details, not the public description. Instructions also support Markdown automatically, without changing stored text. An optional end time earlier than the start means the next day. Use **Manage Schedule** to set or clear times for a single date. End times determine booking completion; without one, bookings complete at the end of the local date. Each program has separate rules, invitations, roster, and calendar. Program coordinators edit only their assigned programs and cannot edit the venue, manage siblings, or assign coordinator roles. Venue coordinators inherit those capabilities across all programs at their venue.

Program settings include **Program Label** and **Participant Label**, defaulting to **Program** and **Participant**. Use singular labels such as **Workshop** and **Instructor** to specialize the interface. Calendars, rosters, invitations, and booking controls use those labels; **Pay** stays unchanged. Labels do not change booking rules: one person per date, one upcoming booking per person per program, and organizer permission for repeat bookings. Gender rules are optional and disabled by default; all accounts require a gender selection. Attendee registration is not implemented.

Use **Calendar invitation lead time (minutes)** in program settings to make Google Calendar invitations begin before the actual session. The default is 0; 15 makes a 10:00 session's invitation start at 9:45. This changes only Google event start times, not session times, booking history, attendance deadlines, or registration invitation emails. Existing upcoming calendar invitations are updated when this setting changes. See [Google Calendar Setup](../google-calendar.md).

Set **First bookable date (optional)** under program **Settings > Details** to open online booking only for sessions on or after that date. For example, enter January 1, 2027 while continuing to manage earlier dates manually: invited participants can accept invitations and book January onward immediately. The calendar initially opens that month, but earlier upcoming sessions remain visible through month navigation. The booking horizon counts calendar months from the later of today or the first bookable date, using the program's time zone; a six-month horizon starting January 1 ends July 1, inclusive. Once that date passes, the horizon rolls forward from today as usual. Leave the date blank to retain today's rolling window. New bookings and date changes, including organizer scheduling, respect the cutoff; existing bookings, attendance confirmations, cancellations, history, and actual session schedules are not rewritten.

Program coordinators use **Calendar** to open its **Calendar**, **Schedule Exceptions**, and **History** sections. From the calendar they can schedule or update bookings, confirm attendance, and manage dated schedule exceptions. The participant group contains **Roster**, **Invitations**, **Applications**, and **History**, which opens **Applications History**. Venue coordinators and system admins can also open **Settings > Coordinators**.

Roster, invitee, and applicant columns use matching portraits and profile links, with placeholders and invitation-detail links for invitees who have not linked an account. Invite to Book has its own roster column. Use the arrow beside a participant to open their last pause note below the row, across the three rightmost columns; it also works without JavaScript.

Filter the roster by **All**, **Available**, **Booked**, **Paused**, or **Inactive**; each option shows its program-wide count. Invitation lists filter by **All**, **Pending**, **Accepted**, **Revoked**, or **Expired**. Pending invitations can be revoked from their detail page as well as the list. **Applications History** filters by event type, uses the same participant portraits, shows events as plain text, and links the person in **By** to their profile. Open its arrow to read private reviewer notes below the event. These filters update in place; without JavaScript, choose a value and press **Filter**.

Use the invitations tab to create personal registration links and view the recipient, status, invited time, sender, expiry, and acceptance time. Invited means the link was created, not confirmation of email delivery; dates and times use the program's time zone. **View** opens the invitation's details without recovering its private link, and **Revoke** is available for Pending invitations. History uses 50-row pages with **Previous** and **Next** links. These links resume from the displayed records rather than a page number, so new invitations do not shift later results; **First** returns to the newest records. The participant roster uses the same navigation with 25 rows and retains **Invite to Book** and **Invite All Available** for granting another booking to existing participants; these are separate from personal registration invitations. Bulk invitations still cover every page.

Invitations provide private, single-use registration links with the same optional-email policy for participants and coordinators. A specified email restricts acceptance to that account; leave it blank for a link one recipient can accept with any account. Participants join by accepting an invitation or having a participation application accepted. Joining grants an initial booking; coordinators can invite participants to book again after they complete a booking. A paused participant can keep an existing booking and cancel it, but cannot book or reschedule until resumed. Memberships and invitations are independent across programs.

Email is used for account recovery and optional invitation delivery. Posebook does not yet send automated booking reminders or booking notifications.
